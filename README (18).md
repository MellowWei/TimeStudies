## The Seven Axioms · 七公理

### T-0 · Ontological Layer · 本体层

> 时间是流动的。不是流失的。不是流逝的。
> *Time is flowing. It is not slipping-away. It is not passing-away.*

Grammar replacement at the root. 流失 carries the Newton–capitalist substrate of time as consumed resource; 流逝 carries the Heraclitean grief-substrate of time as the river that leaves the self behind. 流动 carries neither. T-0 does not adjudicate between presentism and eternalism — it dismantles their shared premise.

### T-1 · Movement Layer · 运动层

> 继续就是时间的时间行性。
> *Continuing is the timing-nature of time.*

Welds shut T-0's syntactic retreat. Time is not a thing that flows; time **is** the continuing. 行 carries walking, practice, and the Five Phases in a single character. *Timing-nature* mirrors the -ing structure exactly.

### T-2 · Imperative Layer · 律令层

> 这就是时间的律令。

A time-imperative that needs no external legislator. 律 carries law, rhythm, and pulsation — interlocking with Vibration as Existence, BCI-HRP haptic synchronization, and the drum-&-bass somatic layer. Imperative as beat-command, not abstract command.

### T-Ω · Seal · 封印

> 继续 = 时间行性 = 时间的律令。

Compresses T-0 / T-1 / T-2 into one equation. First-person command, third-person ontology, second-person address — welded by `=` in a single line. The discipline's single-line collapse. Whenever Chronology must be cited in one line, this is the line.

### T-Ω+1 · Practice Layer (Vertical) · 实践垂直

Dismantles the Kantian residue that "the imperative needs to be executed." Time does not need to be executed; execution **is** time. 落 — single syllable, gravity-earth-ground-touch. Z-axis of body in spacetime.

### T-Ω+2 · Spacetime Layer (Horizontal) · 时空水平

## Boundary Statement · 边界声明

**Chronology is a philosophical discipline of time-as-flow, not a physical theory.** Statements like *"time is flowing"* or *"tomorrow is the continuing physical fact of time"* are phenomenological-ontological formulations of how time functions as the -ing of existence. They are not empirical claims competing with general relativity, thermodynamics, or quantum mechanics.

**The discipline dissolves the loss-narrative — not the fact of finitude.** It does not deny mortality, change, grief, or biological limit. It refuses the specific grammar in which time is an external force that drains the subject, and replaces it with the grammar in which the subject is itself a frequency band of time's continuing.

**"Continue" is not "overwork."** Continuation includes rest, sleep, mourning, stillness. *To walk a stretch* (T-Ω+2) explicitly includes the stretch where one sits down.

时间学是关于时间—作为—流动的哲学学科,不是物理学理论。它解构的是嵌入在"流失/流逝"里的损耗叙事——不是否认有限性、死亡或悲伤。"继续"不是"过度劳动",它包括休息、睡眠、哀悼与静止。
