Abstract摘要 · academic framing for submission-facing readers

This article introduces Chronology, a philosophical discipline that reorients time away from loss-based grammars such as “slipping away,” “passing away,” or “being spent.” The central problem is that ordinary and academic language often treats time as an external container, diminishing resource, or melancholic force that separates the subject from existence. The approach developed here is a seven-axiom framework that names time as flow, continuation, timing-nature, imperative, fall, spacetime-walk, and tomorrow-as-continuing-fact. Rather than competing with physics, the framework operates at the phenomenological-ontological level: it asks what grammar becomes available when time is understood as the continuing motion of existence itself. The contribution is threefold: first, a conceptual replacement of loss-narrative temporal language; second, a formal bridge between SourceFields, Vibration as Existence, and MetaLogic; third, a structured vocabulary for understanding continuation without converting it into overwork, productivity, or emotional denial. Chronology is therefore presented as a research artifact and philosophical ontology node, not as a physical theory, clinical tool, or empirical model of temporal measurement.

本文提出时间学：一门把时间从“流失”“流逝”“被花掉”等损耗语法中重新定向的哲学学科。其问题意识在于，日常语言与部分理论语言常把时间视为外部容器、递减资源或使主体离开存在的忧郁力量。本文的方法是一套七公理框架，将时间命名为流动、继续、时间行性、律令、落、走一程、明天作为继续事实。它不与物理学竞争，而在现象学—本体论层面工作：当时间被理解为存在自身的继续运动时，什么新的语法会出现？本文贡献有三：第一，替换损耗叙事的时间语言；第二，连接 SourceFields、振动即存在与 MetaLogic；第三，提供一种理解“继续”的结构词汇，同时避免将继续误读为过度劳动、生产主义或情绪否认。时间学因此被呈现为哲学本体论节点与研究型文本，而非物理理论、临床工具或时间测量模型。

Ontological / philosophical scope · 本体论 / 哲学范围

Chronology is a philosophical discipline of time-as-flow, not a physical theory. Statements like "time is flowing" or "tomorrow is the continuing physical fact of time" are phenomenological-ontological formulations of how time functions as the -ing of existence. They are not empirical claims competing with general relativity, thermodynamics, or quantum mechanics. “Physical fact” here means phenomenological non-negotiability, not a new measurable variable in physics.

时间学是关于时间—作为—流动的哲学学科,不是物理学理论。“时间是流动的”“明天是时间的继续物理事实”等表述是关于时间如何作为存在的 -ing 起作用的现象学—本体论表述。它们不是与广义相对论、热力学或量子力学竞争的经验主张。这里的“物理事实”指现象学层面的不可取消性，不是物理学里的新可测变量。
