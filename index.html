(i) If a timeless state at the macro-experiential level is equally fundamental and accessible, the imperative is weakened. (ii) If the Block Universe is true, "flow" is an illusion and T-0 dissolves. (iii) If absolute conservation of information precludes genuine novelty, flow = loss. Given its position lock, Chronology resists direct physical falsification; it is a metaphysical / normative framework to be judged by clarity, coherence, and explanatory power.

5. Is-Ought Audit · 是—应当跨越审计

操作方式：将时间的“是”重定义为动词化动作“继续”。对动作结构的描述天然蕴含其完成规范——停跳即不再是心跳，停止即不再是时间。此为语义重定义下的合法跨越，而非逻辑演绎跳跃。与康德绝对命令对照：时间律令是本体前主体的非道德命令；与海德格尔对照：时间学朝向“明天”的继续，是向生律令，而非“向死存在”。

The is-ought crossing is achieved by redefining the "is" of time as the verbal action "continuing." Describing an action‑structure intrinsically entails its norm of completion — a heartbeat that stops is no longer a heartbeat. This is a legitimate crossing via semantic reconstruction, not a deductive leap. Contrast with Kant: the imperative of time is pre‑subjective and non‑moral. Contrast with Heidegger: Chronology faces "tomorrow," a toward‑life imperative rather than being‑toward‑death.

Audit Conclusion · 审计结语

时间学 V0.1 的形式合法性建立在概念创新与语义重定义之上。它成功地将时间的“是”重构为行动，在本体层面合法跨越 is-ought 鸿沟。其最严重的脆弱点在于必须预设 A 系列时间（流动、继续）的本体实在性，这恰是块宇宙理论所要消解的。因此，时间学更接近于一个对人类处境的规范性形而上学框架，其生存力不取决于对物理方程的免疫，而在于它对人类共同经验——走向明天——的深刻锚定。

The formal legality of Chronology V0.1 rests on conceptual innovation and semantic reconstruction. It legitimately crosses the is-ought gap by recasting the "is" of time as action. Its most severe vulnerability is the presupposition of the ontological reality of A‑series time (flow, continuation), which is precisely what the Block Universe dissolves. Thus Chronology is best viewed as a normative metaphysical framework for the human condition, whose survival depends not on immunity to physical equations but on its profound anchoring in shared human experience — walking toward tomorrow.
